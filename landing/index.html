Controlled operations

4 pillars that remove order hallucinations.

  • Persistent memory with SQLite

    Context lives in SQLite, not in the prompt. The prompt stays at about 1.46k tokens from turn 1 to turn 50.

  • Deterministic resolver

    Resolves “those two” and “instead” against known SKUs. Rejects phantom modifiers and allergy conflicts.

  • Kitchen Display System

    Only “place the order” reaches the kitchen. 86’d items and cooking status return to the table on the next turn.

  • Zero dead air

    A one-second spoken acknowledgement starts in about 50 ms while Qwen and Kokoro prepare the full reply.

AssemblyAI Integration

Built around streaming speech.

  • Keyterms prompting54 terms generated from the menu, so “Bun Bo Hue” and “morning glory” come through intact.
  • Tuned turn detectionmin_turn_silence=400, max_turn_silence=1000, end_of_turn_confidence_threshold=0.4.
  • Barge-inSpeechStarted cancels the reply in progress and stops playback on the device.
  • Resilient connectA 5 s handshake timeout with an explicit retry budget: 0 / 5 stalled sessions, down from 3 / 5.
